Cost and insurance

The most common question we get, and the one most chiropractic websites avoid answering.

Insurance

We accept most major plans. Call (636) 856-1260 and we’ll check your benefits before your visit.

Self-pay

Straightforward out-of-pocket pricing. Ask when you request a visit and we’ll quote the new-patient exam and standard visit before you come in.

No long-term contracts

We don't prepay you into a care package. You're treated as long as you need it and no longer.

A note for Missouri Medicaid patients

Missouri Medicaid ended coverage for chiropractic services as of July 1, 2026. If Medicaid was covering your care, call us before your next visit and we'll go through your options.

Common questions

Do I need a referral?

No. Missouri allows direct access to chiropractic care, so you can book with us yourself. If your insurance plan has its own referral requirement we'll help you sort that out.

How many visits will I need?

It depends what the evaluation turns up, and we'll give you a straight estimate after it. What we won't do is sell you a six-month package on day one. When you're better, you're done.

Does an adjustment hurt?

Generally no. Most patients describe relief rather than pain. If you're nervous about being adjusted, tell us — there are gentler techniques and we'll use them.

Can I come in while pregnant?

Yes. We use gentle techniques for pregnancy-related low back, pelvic and sciatic pain.

I was in a car accident. Do you handle that?

Yes. We evaluate and treat auto injuries including whiplash, and we document your care properly for an insurance claim.

How soon can I get in?

Often the same week. Send a request through this page and we'll get back to you with the soonest opening that works for your schedule.
